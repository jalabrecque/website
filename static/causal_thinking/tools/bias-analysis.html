Assumptions and formulas

Both analyses assume a single binary U that does not modify the effect and is independent of the covariates already adjusted for. HRs are treated as RRs, which is reasonable when the outcome is rare.

What would it do (Schlesselman 1978; Lin, Psaty & Kronmal 1998): bias factor = [p₁(RRUY − 1) + 1] / [p₀(RRUY − 1) + 1], applied to the estimate and both CI limits.

What would it take (Ding & VanderWeele 2016; VanderWeele & Ding 2017): the maximum bias is RRAU·RRUY / (RRAU + RRUY − 1). E-value = RR + √[RR(RR − 1)], with RR taken as 1/HR when HR < 1. The CI E-value uses the limit closest to 1, or equals 1 if the CI already includes 1.
