Paste the sentence or sentences where your paper states its aim. Aim Check reports whether an aim statement can be found, and whether its wording points to a descriptive, predictive or causal research question. It is part of EpiCheck, a set of tools for checking the clarity of epidemiological papers.

Everything runs in your browser; nothing you paste is sent or stored. The first load takes 15–30 seconds.
