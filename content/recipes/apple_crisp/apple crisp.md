# Apple crisp

Ingredients

Streusel

- 75 g old-fashioned rolled oats
- 30 g whole wheat flour
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 30 mL pure maple syrup, at room temperature
- 21 g unsalted butter, melted

Filling

- 767 g red apple, diced
- 16 g cornstarch
- 1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/8 tsp ground nutmeg

Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 175C and grease an 8-inch square pan
2. For the streusel, whisk the oats, flour and cinnamon together in a
small bowl. Make a well in the middle, pour in the maple syrup and
melted butter, and stir until everything is evenly combined
3. For the filling, toss the diced apple with the cornstarch, cinnamon
and nutmeg in a large bowl until every piece is coated
4. Tip the filling into the pan and press it down gently with a spatula.
Scatter the topping over it, breaking up any clumps into small pieces so
the apples are covered evenly
5. Bake for 50-60 minutes, until the apples give way easily to a fork
6. Cool to room temperature, then chill for at least 3 hours so the
juices set
