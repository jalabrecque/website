---
title: "Pumpkin Banana Oat Cookies"
---

Soft, naturally sweet cookies for breakfast or a snack.

**Makes:** about 20

## Ingredients

- 120 g pumpkin purée
- 110 g ripe banana, peeled and mashed (about 1 medium)
- 50 g egg (1 medium, without shell)
- 15 g natural peanut butter
- 140 g oat flour
- 4 g baking powder
- 3 g cinnamon
- 40 g mini chocolate chips (optional)

## Method

1. **Mix the wet base.** Mix the pumpkin and banana, then add the egg and peanut butter and mix until combined.
2. **Add the dry ingredients.** Add the oat flour, baking powder and cinnamon (and chocolate chips, if using) and mix to a thick, soft batter.
3. **Rest.** Let the batter sit for 10 minutes so the oat flour absorbs some of the moisture.
4. **Shape.** Scoop about a tablespoon of batter per cookie onto a lined baking sheet. Shape into rounds with the back of a wet spoon and flatten the tops a little; they won't spread much.
5. **Bake** at 175 °C for 16–18 minutes, until set outside but still soft in the centre.
6. **Cool** on the sheet for 10 minutes before moving.

## Notes

- **Convection oven:** 160 °C, check from 13–14 minutes; likely done in 14–16.
- **Oat flour substitutes:** blitz 140 g rolled oats; or 120 g plain flour; 120–130 g whole wheat or spelt flour; 140 g almond flour (batter will be wetter); or 40–45 g coconut flour.
- The batter should be soft and moist, not like regular cookie dough.
- Keeps 3–4 days airtight in the fridge, or freeze up to 2 months.
