Ingredients

- 4-5 carrots, grated
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, to taste
- Pinch of sugar (optional)




Instructions

1. Grate the carrots into a bowl.
2. Add the lemon juice, olive oil, salt and sugar if using.
3. Toss well and let sit for a few minutes before serving so the carrots soften slightly and take on the dressing.
