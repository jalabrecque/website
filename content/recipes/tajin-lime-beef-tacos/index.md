---
title: "Tajín-Lime Ground Beef Tacos"
---

Tangy, lightly spicy beef with a chili-lime kick, ready in about 20 minutes.

**Serves:** 4

## Ingredients

- 500 g ground beef
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 12 g garlic, minced (about 3 cloves)
- 5 g Tajín, plus extra for topping
- 2 g ground cumin
- 16 g tomato paste
- 60 ml water
- 2 limes (1 juiced, 1 cut into wedges)
- 8 small tortillas
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. **Soften the onion.** Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the onion and cook until soft and starting to brown, about 4 minutes.
2. **Brown the beef.** Add the beef, breaking it up, and cook until browned with some crispy bits, about 7 minutes. Drain excess fat if needed.
3. **Season.** Stir in the garlic, Tajín, cumin and tomato paste, and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. **Simmer.** Add the water and simmer until the liquid has mostly cooked off and coats the meat, about 3 minutes. Squeeze in the juice of one lime, then taste and add salt only if needed.
5. **Warm the tortillas** in a dry pan or over a gas flame, about 20 seconds per side.
6. **Assemble.** Fill the tortillas with the beef and top with avocado, cilantro, a sprinkle of extra Tajín and the lime wedges.

## Notes

- Gram amounts for the spices are approximate. Tajín is salty, so start around 4 g and taste before adding more.
- Add Tajín twice: in the pan to flavour the beef, and at the end for a fresh chili-lime hit.
- No tortillas? Serve the beef over rice with the same toppings.
