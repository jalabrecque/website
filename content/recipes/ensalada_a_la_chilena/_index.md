Ingredients

- 4 ripe tomatoes, cut into wedges
- 1 white onion, very thinly sliced
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 2-3 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, to taste
- A few sprigs cilantro, chopped (optional)




Instructions

1. Put the sliced onion in a bowl of cold water with a pinch of salt for about 10 minutes to take the raw edge off, then drain well.
2. Combine the onion and tomatoes in a bowl.
3. Add the lemon juice, olive oil and salt, and toss gently.
4. Scatter over the cilantro if using. Serve right away, before the tomatoes release too much liquid.
