---
title: "Pâté Chinois"
---

The Quebec classic: seasoned beef, a double layer of corn, and buttery mashed potatoes with a browned top.

**Serves:** 6 · **Dish:** 23 × 33 cm (9 × 13 in)

## Ingredients

**Beef**

- 500 g ground beef
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire or HP sauce
- 1/2 tsp dried savory or thyme
- 1 pinch ground cloves or allspice (optional)
- 1 tbsp butter or oil
- Salt and pepper

**Corn**

- 1 can (400–420 g) creamed corn, or 2 cups [homemade cream corn](https://www.jeremylabrecque.org/recipes/cream_corn)
- 1 can (about 300 g drained) corn kernels, or the same amount of frozen corn

**Potatoes**

- 1–1.2 kg floury potatoes (kruimige aardappelen), peeled and cut in chunks
- 60 g butter, plus a little extra for the top
- 125–175 ml warm milk
- 50 g old Gouda or sharp cheddar, grated (optional)
- 2 green onions or a handful of chives, chopped (optional)
- Paprika, for the top

## Method

1. **Cook the potatoes.** Cover with cold salted water, bring to a boil and simmer until very tender, about 20 minutes. Drain well and let them steam dry for a minute.
2. **Mash.** Mash with the butter, then add milk until smooth and spreadable. Stir in the cheese and green onions. Season well with salt and pepper.
3. **Heat the oven** to 190 °C (180 °C fan).
4. **Brown the beef.** Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the onion until soft, about 5 minutes. Add the beef and cook without stirring too much, so it browns properly, 7–8 minutes. Add the garlic, tomato paste, Worcestershire, savory and cloves, and cook 1 minute. Season with salt and pepper.
5. **Char the corn (optional).** Drain the kernels and toss them in a hot dry pan until some are browned, 3–4 minutes. Mix with the creamed corn.
6. **Layer.** Press the beef into the dish, spread the corn over it, then top with the mashed potatoes.
7. **Finish the top.** Rough up the surface with a fork, dot with butter and dust with paprika.
8. **Bake** for 30 minutes, until bubbling at the edges. Grill for the last 3–5 minutes to brown the top. Rest 10 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Creamed corn is hard to find in the Netherlands. Blitz half the kernels with a splash of cream or milk and a knob of butter instead, or use the homemade cream corn above.
- Variations: sautéed mushrooms in the beef, or a thin layer of caramelized onions between the beef and the corn.
- Serve with ketchup, chow chow or pickled beets.
- Assemble ahead and refrigerate up to a day; bake 45–50 minutes from cold.
