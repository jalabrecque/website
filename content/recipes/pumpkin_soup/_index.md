Ingredients

- 1 tbsp butter or oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 900 g pumpkin or butternut squash, peeled and cubed
- 4 cups vegetable or chicken stock
- 1/2 cup cream (optional)
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Pinch of nutmeg (optional)




Instructions

1. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until soft, about 5 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and cook another minute.
3. Add the pumpkin and stock, bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until the pumpkin is very tender, about 20 minutes.
4. Blend until smooth, either with an immersion blender or in batches in a blender.
5. Stir in the cream if using, and season with salt, pepper and nutmeg to taste.
