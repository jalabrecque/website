# Apple crumble

Ingredients

- 300 g plain flour, sieved
- 1 pinch salt
- 175 g unrefined brown sugar
- 200 g unsalted butter, cubed, at room temperature
- 1 knob butter, for greasing

Filling

- 450 g apples, peeled, cored and cubed
- 50 g unrefined brown sugar
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 1 pinch ground cinnamon

Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 180C
2. Mix the flour, salt and 175 g sugar in a large bowl. Add the butter a
few cubes at a time, rubbing it through with your fingertips until the
mixture looks like breadcrumbs
3. Put the apples in a second bowl and scatter over the 50 g sugar, the
tablespoon of flour and the cinnamon. Stir gently so the pieces stay
whole
4. Grease a 24 cm ovenproof dish. Spread the fruit across the base and
cover it with the crumble mixture
5. Bake for 40-45 minutes, until the top has browned and the filling is
bubbling
6. Serve with thick cream or custard
