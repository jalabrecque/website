---
title: "Quiaude"
---

A thick fish and potato soup from the Gaspésie: salt pork, onions, potatoes and cod, simmered together and lightly thickened with flour. Also spelled kiaude or tchaude.

**Serves:** 4–6

## Ingredients

- 150 g salt pork (lard salé), or smoked bacon lardons (spekblokjes), diced
- 2 onions, sliced
- 1 kg waxy or all-purpose potatoes, peeled and cut into thick slices (about 1 cm)
- 700 g skinless cod fillet (kabeljauw), cut into large chunks; haddock or pollock also work
- 1 L water or light fish stock, approximately
- 2 tbsp flour, mixed with 125 ml cold water or milk
- 1/2 tsp dried savory (sarriette)
- Salt and pepper
- Chopped parsley or chives, to serve

## Method

1. **Render the pork.** Cook the salt pork in a large heavy pot over medium heat until golden and the fat has rendered, about 8 minutes.
2. **Soften the onions.** Add the onions and cook in the fat until soft and lightly golden, about 8 minutes.
3. **Add the potatoes.** Add the potatoes, savory and some pepper. Pour in water just to cover. Bring to a boil, then simmer, covered, until the potatoes are almost tender, about 12–15 minutes.
4. **Add the fish.** Lay the cod on top of the potatoes and push it gently into the liquid. Cover and simmer gently until it just flakes, about 8 minutes. Don't stir, or the fish will break up.
5. **Thicken.** Stir the flour mixture again, pour it in around the edges, and gently shake or swirl the pot. Simmer 3–4 minutes until the broth thickens.
6. **Season and serve.** Taste and add salt only at the end, since salt pork varies a lot. Ladle into bowls, keeping the fish in big pieces, and top with parsley or chives. Serve with bread and butter.

## Notes

- Salt cod is traditional. If using it, soak it in cold water in the fridge for 24–36 hours, changing the water 3–4 times, and add little or no salt.
- For a richer version, replace part of the water with milk at the end and warm through without boiling.
- Like most soups, it's better the next day. Reheat gently.
