---
title: "Stovetop Popcorn"
date: 2026-11-01
---

Popcorn in a pot that finishes popping on low or residual heat, so it doesn't scorch.

**Serves:** 3
**Time:** about 10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 90 g popcorn kernels
- 33 g sunflower oil (or another neutral oil, or coconut oil)
- 3–4 g fine salt
- 25 g butter, melted (optional)

## Equipment

- A heavy pot of at least 4 L with a lid

## Method

1. **Heat the oil.** Put the oil and 3 kernels in the pot, cover, and set over medium-high heat. Wait until the test kernels pop (2–3 minutes). The oil is then hot enough.
2. **Add the kernels.** Pour in the rest of the kernels in a single layer, shake the pot to coat them, and cover with the lid slightly ajar so steam can escape. Leave on the heat until popping is steady (about 1 minute).
3. **Finish off the heat.** Turn the heat off (or to low, see notes) and leave the pot covered on the burner, shaking it now and then, until there are 2–3 seconds between pops.
4. **Season.** Tip the popcorn into a large bowl straight away so it doesn't keep cooking. Toss with the salt and, if using, the melted butter.

## Notes

- **Induction:** don't preheat the pot empty, and use medium-high rather than boost. For the finish, turn the zone to low instead of off, since induction stops heating immediately and a lighter pot won't hold enough heat to pop the rest of the kernels.
- **Electric/ceramic hobs and heavy pots** hold heat well, so turning the heat fully off for the finish works.
- Never heat a non-stick pot empty.
