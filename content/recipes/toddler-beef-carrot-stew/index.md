---
title: "Toddler Beef & Carrot Stew (No Salt)"
---

A homemade take on Ella's Kitchen runderstoof: soft, sweet from carrots, and mostly vegetables with a little beef.

**Serves:** 4 toddler portions

## Ingredients

- 100 g ground beef
- 250 g potatoes, peeled and diced small
- 250 g carrots, peeled and diced small
- 60 g parsnip, peeled and diced (optional)
- 40 g onion, finely chopped
- 1 ripe tomato, chopped (or 1 tbsp tomato paste)
- 300 ml water (no stock cube)
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1/4 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp cornstarch, mixed with 1 tbsp cold water
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, very finely chopped (optional)

## Method

1. **Soften the onion.** Warm the oil in a pot over medium-low heat and cook the onion gently until soft and sweet, not browned, about 5 minutes.
2. **Cook the beef.** Add the beef and break it up very finely with a wooden spoon until no pink remains, about 4 minutes. Fine crumbs matter; big chunks are often what toddlers reject.
3. **Simmer.** Add the potatoes, carrots, parsnip, tomato, thyme and water. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer until the carrots are completely soft, about 25 minutes. Stir every 5–10 minutes.
4. **Thicken.** Stir in the cornstarch mixture and simmer another 1–2 minutes until glossy.
5. **Match the texture.** Mash roughly with a potato masher (or pulse briefly with a stick blender) so it's soft with small lumps. Stir in the parsley. Serve lukewarm.

## Notes

- No salt needed: the carrots, parsnip and tomato give the sweetness. Avoid stock cubes, which is where most hidden salt comes from.
- It's fine if the water doesn't fully cover the vegetables; the lid steams them. Add a splash of water if it catches on the bottom.
- No parsnip? Use extra carrot, sweet potato, a little apple, pumpkin or squash, or more potato.
- Keeps 2 days in the fridge, or freeze in portions.
