---
title: "Instant Pot Skyr"
---

Thick, tangy Icelandic-style skyr: skim milk cultured with store-bought skyr, then strained hard.

**Makes:** about 600–700 g

## Ingredients

- 2 L skim milk (magere melk)
- 3 tbsp plain skyr with live cultures, as starter
- 1/8 tsp liquid rennet, diluted in 2 tbsp cool water (optional)

## Equipment

- Instant Pot with a Yogurt function
- Thermometer (handy but not essential)
- Cheesecloth, a thin clean tea towel, or a yogurt strainer holding at least 2 L

## Method

1. **Heat the milk.** Pour the milk into the inner pot, press Yogurt, then Adjust until it shows "Boil" (or use Sauté, stirring often). Heat to 85–90 °C.
2. **Cool to 40 °C.** Remove the inner pot and cool to 40–43 °C, about 30 minutes; an ice bath in the sink speeds this up. Skim off any skin.
3. **Add the starter.** Whisk the starter skyr with a ladle of the warm milk until smooth, then stir it back in. If using rennet, stir it in gently now for about 30 seconds.
4. **Incubate.** Return the pot, close the lid, press Yogurt on Normal and set 10–12 hours. Longer gives more tang. Don't stir or jostle it.
5. **Chill** the set yogurt in the fridge for about 2 hours.
6. **Strain** in the fridge through the lined sieve or strainer for 8–12 hours, until about two-thirds of the volume (1.3–1.4 L) has drained off as whey. Keep the sieve raised above the whey.
7. **Whip and store.** Whisk briefly until smooth and glossy. Keeps about 2 weeks in the fridge. Save 3 tbsp as starter for the next batch.

## Notes

- **Starter:** check the label for "melkzuurbacteriën", "yoghurtcultuur" or "levende culturen", and avoid "warmtebehandeld na fermentatie".
- Without a thermometer: heat until small bubbles form at the edges, then cool until the milk feels comfortably warm to a finger.
- Rennet gives the denser traditional texture; without it you get very thick strained yogurt. Skip it for a first batch.
- Use the whey in bread dough, smoothies, brining, or for up to half the liquid in soups (add near the end).
