---
title: "Hot Chicken"
---

The Quebec diner classic, the easy way: cooked chicken between slices of white bread, smothered in gravy from a packet, with peas on top.

**Serves:** 4

## Ingredients

- 500–600 g cooked chicken, pulled into pieces (rotisserie chicken, leftovers, or 4 chicken breasts/thighs cooked in a pan)
- 2 packets chicken gravy mix (St-Hubert or Club House sauce à hot chicken in Quebec; Knorr or Honig kippenjus in the Netherlands), enough for about 750 ml
- 300 g frozen or canned peas
- 8 slices soft white bread
- Fries, to serve (optional but traditional)

## Method

1. **Make the gravy** as the packet says. Keep it fairly thick; it should coat a spoon.
2. **Warm the chicken.** Stir the chicken into a few spoonfuls of the gravy in a pan, or heat it briefly in the microwave.
3. **Heat the peas** in a little water for 2–3 minutes and drain.
4. **Assemble.** Put a slice of bread on each plate, pile on the chicken, top with a second slice, pour plenty of gravy over everything and finish with the peas. Serve with fries.

## Notes

- A dash of Worcestershire and some black pepper in the packet gravy makes it taste more homemade.
- Good with leftover roast turkey.
